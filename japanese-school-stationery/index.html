Understanding Japanese School Stationery

Japanese stationery is admired around the world. But the most interesting question is not “What should I buy?” It is “Why are these tools used in Japan?”

This five-step cultural roadmap follows the objects on a Japanese study desk in a useful order: paper first, then notebooks, flexible pages, correction, and pencils.
